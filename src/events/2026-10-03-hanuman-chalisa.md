Every Saturday evening the mandir community gathers for eleven recitations of the **Hanuman Chalisa**, followed by Aarti and prasad. All are welcome — no prior experience needed, and printed copies are available.

- **6:30 pm** — Hanuman Chalisa (11 paths)
- **7:15 pm** — Aarti and prasad

Sindoor and chola offerings to Shri Hanuman can be arranged with the priest on the day.
