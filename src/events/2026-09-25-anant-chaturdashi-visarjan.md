On the last day of Ganeshotsav we bid farewell to Bappa with **Uttar Puja**, a final Aarti and a joyful Visarjan procession from the mandir.

## Schedule

- **6:00 pm** — Uttar Puja
- **6:45 pm** — Maha Aarti
- **7:15 pm** — Visarjan procession with dhol

Devotees are welcome to bring their home Ganesh murtis for a collective, eco-friendly visarjan. *Ganpati Bappa Morya, pudhchya varshi lavkar ya!*
