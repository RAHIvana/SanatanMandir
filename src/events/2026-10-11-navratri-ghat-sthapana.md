Sharad Navratri begins with **Ghat Sthapana** at the mandir. Nine nights of Durga Puja, Aarti and Garba follow, culminating in Vijaya Dashami.

## Schedule for the first day

- **5:00 pm** — Ghat Sthapana & Durga Puja
- **6:30 pm** — Aarti
- **7:00 pm** — Garba

## Nightly through October 19

Puja and Aarti at 5:30 pm, Garba and Dandiya from 6:30 pm. Traditional dress is encouraged. Prasad is served every night.

Families may sponsor a night of Garba, the nightly prasad, or the Devi shringar — please contact the mandir.
