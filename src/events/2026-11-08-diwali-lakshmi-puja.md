Celebrate **Diwali** at the mandir with Maha Lakshmi Puja, Aarti, diya lighting and mithai prasad.

## Schedule

- **6:30 pm** — Maha Lakshmi Puja
- **7:30 pm** — Aarti
- **7:45 pm** — Diya lighting on the mandir grounds and prasad

Families may bring their own Lakshmi–Ganesh murtis and account books (chopda) for puja. Diyas are available at the mandir for a small donation.
