On the day after Diwali the mandir celebrates **Govardhan Puja** with an Annakut — a mountain of food offerings before the deities — followed by a community dinner.

- **5:30 pm** — Govardhan Puja
- **6:15 pm** — Annakut Aarti and darshan
- **7:00 pm** — Community dinner (Mahaprasad)

Please sign up to bring a dish for the Annakut; sweets, savouries, rice and vegetable preparations are all welcome. Contact the mandir kitchen team through the WhatsApp group.
