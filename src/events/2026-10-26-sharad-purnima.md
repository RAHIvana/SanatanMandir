On the night of **Sharad Purnima** the mandir hosts a Satyanarayan Katha followed by Aarti. Kheer is offered to the moon and served as prasad.

- **6:00 pm** — Satyanarayan Katha
- **7:15 pm** — Aarti
- **7:30 pm** — Kheer prasad

Families who wish to be the yajman for the katha may contact the priest.
