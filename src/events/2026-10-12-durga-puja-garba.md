Nightly **Durga Puja, Aarti and Garba** during Navratri, from October 12 to October 19.

- **5:30 pm** — Puja & Aarti
- **6:30 pm** — Garba & Dandiya

Traditional dress encouraged. Prasad served nightly. Volunteers are needed for parking, prasad and clean-up — sign up at the mandir desk or on the Weekend Seva page.
