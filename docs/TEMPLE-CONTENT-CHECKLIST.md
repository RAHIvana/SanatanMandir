# Content needed from the temple

Everything on this list currently shows as a yellow **PLACEHOLDER** box on the website, or is a best guess taken from the old website. Send answers in any form (WhatsApp message, email, Word document) — they will be dropped into the site.

## Must-have before launch

1. **Zelle and Venmo** — the QR code images and the exact IDs to show under them (Donate page). The old site's Zelle QR is kept in case it is still current.
2. **Square donation link** — confirm the existing card-payment link is still the right one, or send a new one.
3. **Regular schedule** — daily aarti times and any weekly programs (Hanuman Chalisa, bhajans, Sunday puja) with times (Events page).
4. **The real event list** for the coming months (title, date, time, a few lines of description, flyer if any). The site currently shows sample events marked "Sample"; each real event becomes a file per `ADDING-EVENTS.md`.
5. **Google Forms** (six short forms; a suggested field list for each is below). Only the link is needed.
6. **Photos of 2–3 past events** to launch the Gallery (a shared Google Photos album link is enough).
7. **Photo of the Shri Krishna murti** (and any other deity not yet shown) for the home page — currently a "photo coming soon" placeholder. Existing deity photos are oval crops; a straight-on photo of each murti works best.

## Should-have

8. **Committee roles** — a title for each committee member (President, Secretary, Treasurer, Cultural, Kitchen, …). The About page currently shows names only. Confirm whether Dr. Sagar Pandya's phone number should stay on the About page.
9. **Weekend Seva, Monthly Seva, Kids Seva, Vidya Gurukulam** — what each is, when it runs, who coordinates it, how to join (each has its own page with draft text to replace).
10. **Wishlist** — items the mandir needs, with approximate cost or a store link (Donate page).
11. **Non-profit status** — is the mandir a registered 501(c)(3)? If yes, the EIN, so the Donate page can say donations are tax-deductible.
12. **Priest services** — Shastriji to review the 26 service descriptions, add/remove services, and say whether suggested dakshina amounts should be shown.
13. **Our Story** — confirm the timeline on the About page: opening date (2023), Ram Parivar Pran Pratishtha (January 2024?), the move to 281 Lemens Ave, Hutto.

## Nice-to-have

14. A good **photo of the mandir building / entrance** for the About page and social sharing.
15. **Newsletter** — who will send it and from which address.
16. Any **Hindi/Gujarati** wording preferences (the header shows श्री सनातन मंदिर).

## Suggested Google Form fields

Create each at https://forms.google.com signed in as the temple account, then **Send → < > (embed) → copy** the `src` link, or just the normal share link.

- **Priest service request**: Name · Phone · Email · Service (dropdown: the 26 services) · Preferred date · Preferred time · At the mandir or at home (address) · Notes.
- **Newsletter**: Name · Email · Phone (optional, for WhatsApp).
- **Weekend Seva**: Name · Phone · Email · Preferred day (Sat/Sun) · How often · Skills or interests · Notes.
- **Monthly Seva**: Name · Phone · Email · Month(s) wanted · Seva type (puja sponsorship / prasad / maintenance / other) · Notes.
- **Kids Seva**: Parent name · Phone · Email · Child's name and age · Availability · Notes.
- **Vidya Gurukulam**: Parent/student name · Phone · Email · Student age · Class(es) of interest · Notes.
